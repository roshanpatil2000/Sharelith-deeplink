Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Sharelith helps you split expenses with friends and keep track of who owes whom. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Sharelith app and website, why we collect it, who it's shared with, and the choices you have.

Usage analytics isn't in the app yet; it's marked "when available". This policy will be updated before it's added.

Information you give us

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we can't read them.
  • Your profile: optionally a phone number and a profile photo, plus your default currency and time zone.
  • What you add in the app: your friends, groups, expenses, how each expense is split, payments you record, reminders you send, and a history of these changes.
  • Receipt photos (Pro): a photo of a bill you attach to an expense.
  • People you add: when you add a friend by email who isn't on Sharelith yet, we store their email address and the name you give, and send them one invitation email.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a token from Google's push service so we can notify you about expenses, payments, reminders and groups that involve you.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, technical details such as your device model, operating system version, app version and what the app was doing, linked to your account ID (not your name or email).
  • Performance: how long the app takes to start and to load data, with your device model, operating system, app version, country (derived from your IP address) and network type. It isn't linked to your account.
  • App settings from our servers: to switch features on or off and tell you when an update is needed, the app fetches settings using an app instance identifier and your app version.
  • Usage analytics (when available): how the app is used, such as which screens are opened and which features are used, along with device and app details and an app instance identifier. We use this to understand what's useful and what to improve.
  • Advertising: to show ads, our ad partner may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads.
  • Sign-in details: each time you sign in, the time, your device model, operating system and app version, your IP address and an approximate location (city, region and country) worked out from that IP address. See "Sign-in security" below.

Contacts: if you choose "Choose from contacts" when adding a friend, the app asks for permission and reads names and email addresses on your phone so you can pick someone. Your contacts stay on your phone: only the one person you pick is sent to us, the same as typing their email yourself. We never upload your address book. You can turn this permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Invite links: when you create an invite link or QR code, we store a random code linked to your account (and to a group, for a group invite) for 30 days. Anyone who opens it becomes your friend on Sharelith, and joins the group for a group invite, so share it only with people you know.

We don't access your photos (other than one you choose as your profile photo) or precise location.

Sign-in security

Sharelith stays signed in on one device at a time. When you sign in on a new device, your other devices are signed out and get a notification saying so.

After every sign-in we email you a sign-in alert with the details listed above, so you can spot a sign-in that wasn't you and reset your password. To find the approximate location, we send only the IP address to our location provider (ipinfo.io); the location is estimated and may not be exact. We use these details to send the alert and don't keep them ourselves; our sign-in provider keeps its own security logs of sign-ins, including IP addresses.

Ads

The free version of Sharelith shows ads, served by a third-party ad network. Where the law requires it, we'll ask for your consent before showing personalised ads; otherwise you'll see non-personalised ads based on context. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device's settings at any time. Ads never use your expenses, balances or the people you share them with.

Sharelith Pro

Sharelith Pro is a paid subscription, monthly or yearly, sold through Google Play on Android (iPhone is coming later). It removes ads and adds receipt photos, export and spending insights. We may also turn Pro on for some accounts at no charge, for example for testing.

  • Payments: Google Play takes your payment; we never see your card or bank details. When you subscribe, the app sends us the purchase reference, which we check with Google. From Google we receive only what we need to run Pro: whether the subscription is active, its plan, when it renews or ends, and the purchase reference. Google tells us when it renews, is cancelled or is refunded. Manage or cancel it in Google Play.
  • Receipt photos: stored privately with the expense. Everyone on that expense can see the photo; nobody else can. Removing it deletes the file.
  • Export: CSV and PDF files are made on your device from expenses you can already see, and go only where you choose to share them. We don't keep a copy.
  • Insights: worked out on our servers from your own expenses (your share, what you paid, categories and months) and shown only to you.

Who can see your information

  • Your friends and the members of your groups see your name, email address and profile photo.
  • People on an expense see that expense, who paid and what each person owes. Members of a group see the group's expenses and activity.
  • Your phone number, time zone and other settings are visible only to you.

How we use it

  • To run Sharelith: work out balances, show your activity, keep your devices in sync and send notifications.
  • To send account emails: confirming your email, resetting your password, telling you when someone adds you.
  • To keep the app working and secure: fix crashes, prevent abuse, limit spam (such as reminders to the same person), keep you signed in on one device at a time and email you an alert after each sign-in.
  • To improve the app, using analytics when available.
  • To show ads in the free version, and to provide Pro features to subscribers.

We don't sell your personal information.

Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: performance of our contract with you, to provide the app; our legitimate interests, to keep it secure, fix problems and improve it; your consent, for personalised ads and any optional analytics, which you can withdraw at any time (in the app under Account → Ad choices, where offered); and legal obligations where they apply.

Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in and file storage, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google Firebase: Cloud Messaging delivers notifications; Crashlytics receives crash reports; Performance Monitoring measures speed; Remote Config delivers app settings; App Distribution sends test versions to testers who sign up for them.
  • Mailtrap: sends account emails and sign-in alerts.
  • ipinfo.io: estimates an approximate location from the IP address of a sign-in, for the sign-in alert. It receives only the IP address.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and the links in our emails.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the free version. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks before ads are personalised; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • An analytics provider (when available): we'll name it here before it is added.
  • Google Play: sells and bills the Pro subscription, and tells us whether it's active.

These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms. Some of them may process it outside your country, including in the United States, with safeguards required by law.

On your device

Your sign-in session is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). A copy of your recent data is cached on the device so the app opens quickly. The cache is encrypted (AES-256) with a key kept in that secure storage, and it's removed when you log out.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and performance data for up to 90 days by our providers. Analytics data, when available, will be kept for no more than 14 months.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account. You need to settle up first, so nobody is left with a balance they can't clear.

When you delete your account, your sign-in, email address, phone number, photo and notification tokens are removed. Expenses you shared, and receipt photos on them, stay in other people's records, shown under "Deleted user", so their balances remain correct. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription: cancel it in Google Play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Most of these you can do in the app; for anything else, email us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited so people see only what they share. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Children

Sharelith isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, such as when analytics is added, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
